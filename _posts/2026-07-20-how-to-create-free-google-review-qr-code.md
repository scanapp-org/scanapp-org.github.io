For local businesses, customer reviews are one of the most powerful ranking factors in Google’s map pack. The more positive, recent reviews your business receives, the higher you will appear when potential customers search for services near them.

The challenge is getting customers to actually leave feedback. Most people are willing to write a review, but they don't want to search for your business on Google Maps, find the review tab, and click through several screens.

A **Google Review QR code** solves this by bridging the physical-digital gap. Customers simply scan the code with their smartphone at checkout and are instantly taken to the review page.

Here is a step-by-step guide on how to create a Google Review QR code for free using **ScanApp.org**.

---

## Step 1: Find Your Google Business Review Link

To make the process as seamless as possible, you must link directly to the review entry form, not just your business home page. 

1. Sign in to the Google account associated with your **Google Business Profile**.
2. Search for your business name on Google Search. You should see your profile dashboard.
3. Click the **"Ask for reviews"** button on the dashboard.
4. Google will display a pop-up window with a shortened link (e.g., `https://g.page/r/Cw9.../review`).
5. Copy this URL.

> [!NOTE]
> If you don't have access to your Google Business Profile dashboard, search for your business on Google Maps, click the "Write a review" button, and copy the long URL from your browser's address bar. It will work exactly the same way.

---

## Step 2: Generate the QR Code on ScanApp

Once you have copied the review link, you can create the visual QR code:

1. Open **[scanapp.org/generator](/generate.html)** (or navigate to the generator section) in your browser.
2. Paste the copied review URL into the input field.
3. Customize the design:
   * **Color**: Choose high-contrast colors (classic black-on-white is best for scanning reliability under dim lighting).
   * **Logo**: Add your business logo to the center of the QR code to build trust.
   * **Size**: Ensure the quiet zone (the blank margin around the code) remains clear.
4. Download the generated code as a high-resolution **PNG** or **SVG** vector file (ideal for high-quality printing).

---

## Step 3: Best Practices for Printing and Placement

Having the QR code is only half the battle; where you put it determines your conversion rates:

* **Include a Call to Action (CTA)**: Don't print the QR code alone. Add text around it like: *"Love our service? Scan to leave a Google Review!"* or *"Tell us how we did!"*
* **Strategic Locations**:
  * **Checkout Counter**: Place a small acrylic stand with the QR code next to the register.
  * **Receipts**: Add the QR code to the bottom of printed receipts.
  * **Table Tents**: If you run a restaurant, place them on tables.
  * **Business Cards**: Hand a review card to customers after completed services (e.g., HVAC repair, haircuts).
* **Verify Before Printing**: Always scan the printed proof with your phone camera to make sure it opens the correct review window before printing in bulk.
