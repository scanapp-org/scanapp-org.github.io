Logging into WhatsApp Web on a computer normally requires you to open the WhatsApp app on your phone, navigate to settings, and scan the QR code displayed on your computer screen.

But what if your phone's camera is cracked, scratched, blurry, or completely broken? 

Thankfully, you don't need a functioning camera to log in. WhatsApp offers an official alternative login workflow called **"Link with Phone Number"** that uses an OTP (One-Time Password) code instead.

Here is the step-by-step guide to linking your device without scanning a QR code.

---

## Step-by-Step: Link WhatsApp Web with Your Phone Number

This official method works on both Android and iOS devices:

1. **Open WhatsApp Web**: On your computer, open your web browser and go to [web.whatsapp.com](https://web.whatsapp.com).
2. **Click the Alternative Link**: Below the QR code displayed on the screen, click the text that says **"Link with phone number"**.
3. **Select Your Country**: Choose your country from the dropdown list to automatically insert the correct country code.
4. **Enter Your Phone Number**: Type your mobile phone number in the field and click **Next**.
5. **Get Your Code**: WhatsApp Web will display an **8-character code** (letters and numbers) on your computer screen.
6. **Open Your Phone**: Tap the WhatsApp notification on your phone or open the WhatsApp app.
7. **Enter the Code**:
   * Navigate to **Settings** → **Linked Devices** → **Link a Device**.
   * When the camera scanning screen opens, look at the bottom and tap **"Link with phone number instead"**.
   * Type the 8-character code shown on your computer screen.
8. **Confirmation**: Your browser will automatically refresh and sync your messages.

## Troubleshooting Focus and Glare Issues (If You Must Scan)

If you are trying to use a camera that is only slightly damaged or has trouble focusing:

* **Clean the Lens**: Use a microfiber cloth to clean finger grease off the camera lens.
* **Reduce Screen Glare**: Tilt your computer screen slightly to prevent overhead lights or windows from reflecting off the QR code, which confuses the scanner.
* **Maintain the Correct Distance**: Keep your phone about 10–12 inches away from the monitor and let it focus automatically. Don't hold the camera too close.
* **Scale the Browser Window**: Press `Ctrl + minus (-)` on Windows or `Cmd + minus (-)` on Mac to make the QR code smaller, which can help wide-angle cameras focus more easily.
