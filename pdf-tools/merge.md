## Combine PDF files
1. Select Open PDFs and choose the documents in your intended order.
2. Check the page list. Add PDFs / merge appends more documents; Move earlier and Move later change page order.
3. Leave Export pages empty to include all pages and download the edited PDF.

## What happens to forms?
Supported form fields are flattened into visible page content during export. Fill their values in the form panel first. The exported merged document is not an interactive form. Existing bookmarks, document-level attachments and metadata are not promised to survive a merge.

## Review the output
Check page order and filled fields in the downloaded file before sharing. Password-protected PDFs and signature-field documents are not supported.
