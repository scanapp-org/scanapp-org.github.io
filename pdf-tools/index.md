## Edit a PDF from opening to export
Open your PDF or try the three-page sample. Select a page, choose Add text, Highlight, Draw or Place image, and click the preview to position your edit. Use Undo or Redo to review changes. Download edited PDF, then open the downloaded file to verify the result.

## What does this editor change?
Add notes, images and drawn signatures over existing pages. Rearrange, rotate or delete pages and combine documents. Existing embedded text is not rewritten. For flat forms, place added text; for supported interactive forms, use the form-fields panel.

## Keep your original
Your original files are never overwritten. Unsaved edits disappear when you clear or close the tab. Export a copy before leaving.
