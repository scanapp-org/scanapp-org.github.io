## Rearrange your document
Select a page in the page list. Move earlier or Move later changes its place in the output. Rotate 90° turns only that page clockwise. Delete page removes it from the output; at least one page must remain.

## Reverse a change
Undo and Redo cover page changes, added content and supported form-value changes. History keeps the last 30 changes in this tab. Your source file is unchanged until you separately choose what to do with the exported copy.

## Check page orientation
The preview reflects the saved page rotation. Review pages and export all pages, or specify a subset in Export pages.
