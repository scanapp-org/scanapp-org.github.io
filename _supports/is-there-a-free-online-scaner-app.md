Many people who want to scan barcodes or QR codes search for a free online **scaner** or **skaner** app. While these are common misspellings of "scanner", they represent a huge demand for quick, lightweight web-based scanning solutions.

In this article, we explain how you can scan any QR code or barcode instantly using [ScanApp](https://scanapp.org) without needing to install any software.

## What is a web-based scaner app?

A web-based scaner (scanner) is an application that runs entirely inside your web browser (like Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge). Instead of downloading a heavy app from the App Store or Google Play Store, you simply visit a URL and start scanning using your device's built-in camera or by uploading image files.

## How to scan QR codes with ScanApp

ScanApp is a 100% free online scaner that works on PC, Mac, laptop, Android, and iOS/iPhone. To scan a code:

1. Open your browser and go to [scanapp.org](https://scanapp.org).
2. Grant camera permissions when prompted.
3. Point your camera at the QR code or barcode.
4. The scan results will appear instantly!

## Scanning QR codes from images, photos, or screenshots

If you have a QR code saved as an image file, photo, or screenshot in your gallery, you don't need a physical camera to scan it:

1. Open [ScanApp](https://scanapp.org) in your browser.
2. Drag and drop the image file anywhere on the screen, or click the upload icon to select a photo from your gallery.
3. ScanApp will decode the QR code or barcode locally on your device.

## Why use ScanApp instead of installing an app?

- **Fully Private**: Scanning is done locally. Your camera feed or scanned data is never sent to any server.
- **No Install or Signup**: Works instantly without registration or downloading.
- **Cross-Platform**: Compatible with Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux.
