Supported documents and limits

Use unprotected PDFs up to 40 MB and 200 pages combined. Password-protected PDFs, XFA forms and documents with digital-signature fields are rejected. Complex documents may have unsupported features; keep the original and inspect your exported copy.

This editor adds content and changes pages. It does not rewrite existing embedded text, perform OCR, securely redact information, or provide certificate signing. Highlights and added images never remove underlying information.

Does it really work offline?

Select Enable offline use while connected and wait for Ready offline. Then reopen a bookmarked PDF tool page without a connection. Editing, form filling and exporting use local code. The first setup needs the internet, and clearing browser storage can remove the offline installation. Documents are not uploaded or automatically saved.
